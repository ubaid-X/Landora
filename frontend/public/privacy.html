Privacy Policy

Last updated: 09 October 2026

1. Introduction

At Property Dealing, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and what rights you have.

By using our Platform, you consent to the practices described in this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

From Dealers (Registration)

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password (stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash — never in plain text)
  • Business name
  • Owner name
  • Phone number
  • WhatsApp number (optional)
  • Full address
  • Province and City

From Dealers (Property Listings)

  • Property title and description
  • Price, area size, and area unit
  • Property type
  • Full address (private — admin only)
  • Province and City (public)
  • Google Maps location URL
  • Property images
  • Sold / Unsold status

From Public Users

  • We do not require registration or login for public users.
  • We do not collect names, emails, or phone numbers from public visitors.
  • When you contact us by phone or email, we may record your name and contact details for the purpose of connecting you with a dealer.

Automatically Collected

  • PHP session cookies (only for logged-in dealers and admins)
  • Basic server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamp) — kept short-term for security

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage Dealer accounts
  • Display property listings to public users
  • Verify dealer legitimacy before approving accounts
  • Connect interested users with dealers
  • Communicate with you about your account or listings
  • Improve the Platform's features and security
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. What Is Visible to Whom

We carefully control who can see what information:

Public Users See

  • Property title and description
  • Price, area, and property type
  • City and Province
  • Sold / Unsold status
  • Property images
  • Dealer's business name
  • The Platform's contact number

Public Users Do Not See

  • Full property address
  • Google Maps URL
  • Dealer's phone or WhatsApp number
  • Dealer's email or full address

Admins See

  • All information provided by dealers and users
  • Private fields required for verification and communication

5. Cookies & Sessions

We use a single PHP session cookie. It is created only when a dealer or admin logs in and is destroyed when they log out or after a period of inactivity.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies.

6. Third-Party Services

The Platform loads the following third-party libraries and services from public CDNs to make the site work:

  • Bootstrap CDN — for layout and styling
  • Font Awesome CDN — for icons
  • Leaflet + OpenStreetMap — for the location picker and map tiles
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) — for address search on the location picker

These services may see your IP address and browser information when their resources load. We do not send them any of your personal information from our database.

7. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored on our secured MySQL database.
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard PHP hashing — never stored in plain text.
  • Sensitive endpoints are protected by authentication and role-based access controls.
  • Uploaded images are stored outside the application code and served via static URLs.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Use strong passwords and keep them confidential.

8. Data Retention

  • Dealer accounts and their listings remain in our database until deleted by the admin.
  • When the admin deletes a dealer account, all associated properties and images are also permanently deleted (cascade delete).
  • Rejected dealers are not automatically removed — admin decides.
  • Server logs are kept for a short period for security and debugging purposes, then discarded.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Ask questions about how your data is used

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the details below.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Platform is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Platform after any change means you accept the new Policy.
